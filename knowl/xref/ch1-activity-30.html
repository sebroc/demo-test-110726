Activity 1.3.1.

Solve the homogeneous system
\begin{equation*} \begin{array}{rrr} 2x_1 + 4x_3 + 6x_4 & = & 0 \\ x_1 + 2x_2 + 7x_4 & = & 0 \end{array} \end{equation*}
🔗
Solution.
Augmented matrix:
\begin{equation*} \left[ \begin{array}{cccc|c} 2 & 0 & 4 & 6 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 & 7 & 0 \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
🔗
Gaussian elimination:
\begin{equation*} R_1 \to \frac{1}{2}R_1 \implies \left[ \begin{array}{cccc|c} 1 & 0 & 2 & 3 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 & 7 & 0 \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} R_2 \to R_2 - R_1 \implies \left[ \begin{array}{cccc|c} 1 & 0 & 2 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & -2 & 4 & 0 \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} R_2 \to \frac{1}{2}R_2 \implies \left[ \begin{array}{cccc|c} 1 & 0 & 2 & 3 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 2 & 0 \end{array} \right] \end{equation*}
🔗
Parametric form of the solutions:
\begin{equation*} x_1 = -2x_3 - 3x_4, \quad x_2 = x_3 - 2x_4, \quad x_3 = x_3, \quad x_4 = x_4 \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \text{Let } x_3 = s, \, x_4 = t \text{ (free variables).} \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} x_1 &= -2s - 3t, \\ x_2 &= s - 2t, \\ x_3 &= s, \\ x_4 &= t. \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
